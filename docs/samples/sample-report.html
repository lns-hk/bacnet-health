8Methodology and limitations

How the assessment works

bacnet-health combines two kinds of observation. Passive observation listens to BACnet/IP traffic (live, or from a packet capture file) and measures broadcast rates, storms, errors and BBMD forwarding without sending anything. Active observation sends a small, rate-limited set of read-only requests: ranged Who-Is to discover devices, ReadProperty(-Multiple) to read the Device object, and Read-BDT / Read-FDT to audit BBMDs. The tool never writes to a device or changes its configuration. When both are done, listening comes first so that the tool's own traffic does not distort the baseline, and the tool's own frames are excluded from all passive metrics.

Severity, confidence and vantage

SeverityMeaning
CriticalObserved, with a direct security exposure or a control disruption happening during the capture.
HighLikely to affect control or security now.
MediumDegrades reliability or maintainability.
LowHygiene: worth fixing during normal maintenance.
InfoInventory facts and context; no action required.

Confidence is Observed when the problem was seen directly in traffic or responses, and Inferred when it was deduced from other evidence (for example from BBMD tables). Inferred findings are never rated Critical. Vantage is where the traffic was observed from: self (the tool's own socket), access (an ordinary switch port) or span (a mirror port). Each check needs a minimum vantage; checks that could not be performed are listed in section 5. There is deliberately no overall numeric score.

Thresholds

Thresholds marked provisional have not yet been calibrated with field data. They are conservative starting values chosen from the BACnet standard and engineering judgement, and they will be revised as field captures become available. A finding that depends on a provisional threshold is a prompt to investigate, not a measured fault. The raw metrics are kept in the JSON result so that findings can be re-evaluated later.

Limitations

  • A passive observation only covers the time it ran. Intermittent problems outside that window are not seen.
  • From a self or access vantage, device-to-device unicast traffic (including writes) is not visible.
  • Devices behind BACnet routers are recorded from their I-Am, but are not read unless explicitly enabled.
  • BACnet/IPv6 (Annex U), BACnet Secure Connect and MS/TP links that do not reach the IP network are out of scope for this version.
  • The capture covered 10 minutes during a weekday morning.

bacnet-health 0.1 is a pre-release and has not yet been validated on physical controllers.
